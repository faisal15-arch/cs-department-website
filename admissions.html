Admissions

Begin your journey with the Department of Computer Science

Degree Programs

🎓

BSCS

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science — 4-year program (130 credit hours). Covers software, AI, networks, databases, and more.

🔬

MSCS

Master of Science in Computer Science — 2-year research or coursework program for graduates with a CS background.

📖

PhD CS

Doctor of Philosophy in Computer Science — 3–5 year research degree with focus on original contributions to the field.

Eligibility Criteria

  • BSCS: Minimum 60% marks (or Grade C) in FSc Pre-Engineering / ICS (Computer Science) or equivalent A-levels.
  • NTS / Entry Test: Valid NTS-NAT score OR COMSATS University Admission Test (CUAT) is required.
  • MSCS: 16 years of education (BS/BE in CS or related field) with minimum CGPA 2.5 out of 4.0.
  • PhD CS: MS degree in CS or related discipline with minimum CGPA 3.0 out of 4.0 and a research proposal.
  • Documents Required: Matric + Intermediate certificates, CNIC/B-Form, passport photos, and entry test score card.

How to Apply

1

Register Online

Create an account at the COMSATS admissions portal at admission.comsats.edu.pk

2

Fill Application

Complete the online application form with your personal and academic information.

3

Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of all required academic certificates and ID documents.

4

Pay Fee

Pay the application processing fee via the designated bank or online payment gateway.

5

Take Entry Test

Appear for the NTS-NAT or COMSATS University Admission Test on the scheduled date.

6

Merit List

Check the merit list published on the admissions portal and confirm your seat.
